Demonstrated sync defect: In isolated SQLite memory with synthetic records, staff A's PHP 245 income transaction was submitted to SyncController::sync() by staff B using its client_id. It became a PHP 1 expense owned by staff B. The controller matches client_id and business_id but does not enforce the original staff owner. This establishes a same-business authorization and financial-integrity defect, not a cross-business leak. Evidence: web/app/Http/Controllers/API/SyncController.php, sync().

4. TEN PRIORITIZED FIXES

1) Secure sync writes. Why: prevents demonstrated cross-staff overwrite. Files: API/SyncController.php and API/TransactionController.php. Effort: S-M. Verify staff B cannot change staff A's record and rejected requests leave all fields unchanged.
2) Reject ambiguous OCR totals. Why: cash tendered can become the recorded amount. Files: OCRService.js and ReceiptScannerScreen.js. Effort: M. Verify split-line TOTAL 245/CASH 500 yields 245 or review/error, never automatic 500.
3) Separate notification matching from payment confirmation or require authorized staff verification. Why: a matching device signal alone can commit a sale. Files: API/OrderController.php and OrderQueueScreen.js. Effort: M. Verify a fabricated notification cannot commit a sale and audit metadata matches actual verification.
4) Repair fixtures and investigate remaining test failures. Why: the suites are not a reliable regression gate. Files: web/tests/TestCase.php and affected PHP/Jest tests. Effort: M. Verify full suites pass without weakening assertions or security.
5) Add test/lint CI gates before distribution. Why: the release workflow does not run application tests. Files: .github/workflows/. Effort: S. Verify failing Jest or Laravel tests prevent a release.
6) Quarantine rejected wallet captures. Why: one bad item may block later valid uploads. Files: WalletCaptureSync.js, WalletCaptureStore.java, wallet API controller. Effort: M. Verify one invalid item is isolated while later captures sync once.
7) Persist PIN lockout and define recovery. Why: restart resets attempt accounting. Files: StaffPinGate.js and staffPinStorage.js. Effort: M. Verify restart does not reset failed attempts and recovery checks identity.
8) Add stock-adjustment idempotency. Why: retry after a lost response can adjust stock twice. Files: API/StockController.php and mobile caller. Effort: M. Verify identical retried requests change stock once.
9) Preserve original trusted-device MFA expiry. Why: restoring trust near expiry writes a fresh verified timestamp. Files: OwnerTrustedDeviceService.php and EnsureOwnerAccountSecurity.php. Effort: S-M. Verify restoration near day 30 cannot create another 30-day MFA allowance. Actual browser-session lifetime may shorten the effect; this finding is static.
10) Standardize stock/recipe precision and test MySQL. Why: some calculations round to four decimals while quantities are stored at two. Files: quantity migrations, models, order financial logic. Effort: M. Verify fractional deductions and reversals reconcile exactly.
